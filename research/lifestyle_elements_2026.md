## WORKSPACE/CAFE AESTHETIC (The "Aspirational Accessibility" Formula)

### The Viral Cafe Shot (Alici.ai Formula)
| Element | Specification | Why It Works |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| **Color Harmony** | 3-color palette locked (White shirt + light blue pants + pink accents) | Visual cohesion, brand recognition |
| **Subject Placement** | 60% frame, off-center (rule of thirds intersection) | Natural visual flow, not dead center |
| **Lighting Direction** | Soft daylight from upper left, minimal shadows | Flattering, approachable |
| **Prop Storytelling** | Laptop + coffee + clutch = work-life balance | Each object reinforces narrative |
| **Texture Contrast** | Smooth marble vs woven chairs vs fluffy sandals | Visual interest without clutter |

### Cafe Selection Criteria (WomenGuide 2026)
- ✅ **Female-friendly vibe** (not stale beer/dude-bro energy)
- ✅ **Natural light** (windows, not fluorescent)
- ✅ **WiFi speed** (tested, screenshot Speedtest)
- ✅ **Table depth** (room for laptop + drink + notebook)
- ✅ **Background** (clean, no private conversations visible)
- ✅ **Aesthetic** (photogenic but not staged)
- ✅ **Female-owned/staffed** bonus

## LOCATION-SPECIFIC VISUAL LIBRARIES

### LISBON — "Organized Chaos, Historic Charm"
**Color Palette:** Terracotta roofs, yellow trams, blue tiles, white cobblestones, green hills
**Key Visual Anchors:**
- **Miradouros** (viewpoints): Santa Catarina, São Pedro de Alcântara, Graça
- **Tram 28:** Yellow, crowded, iconic, moving through narrow streets
- **Azulejos:** Blue tile facades, close-up patterns, whole building
- **Fado houses:** Red curtains, intimate, candlelit
- **Pastelarias:** Queue of locals, pastel de nata, espresso
- **Tagus river:** Sunset, 25 de Abril bridge, sailboats
- **Alfama laundry:** Clotheslines between buildings, wind, color
- **Calçada portuguesa:** Wavy stone patterns, worn smooth

### BALI — "Tropical Rhythm, Spiritual Undercurrent"
**Color Palette:** Emerald rice fields, volcanic black sand, turquoise water, frangipani white/pink, incense smoke
**Key Visual Anchors:**
- **Rice terraces:** Tegalalang, Jatiluwih, Sidemen — layers of green
- **Temples:** Tanah Lot (sea), Uluwatu (cliff), Tirta Empul (water)
- **Scooter life:** Helmet, rice field path, temple offerings on handlebars
- **Warungs:** Plastic stools, nasi campur, sambal, family cooking
- **Ceremonies:** Canang sari (offerings), gamelan, white/yellow clothing
- **Waterfalls:** Sekumpul, Gitgit, Tukad Cepung — light beams
- **Beach clubs:** Daybeds, coconut, sunset, minimal aesthetic
- **Volcanoes:** Batur sunrise, Agung majestic, steam vents
