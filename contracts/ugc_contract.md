## NEGOTIATION SCRIPTS

### "We don't have budget for Spark Ads"
> "No problem. Organic only is $[X]. Spark Ads rights can be added later at $[Y]/30 days if performance warrants."

### "We need exclusivity"
> "Exclusivity is 2-3x base rate for 30 days. For [category] only, it's $[X]. Full category exclusivity is $[Y]. Which do you need?"

### "We want perpetual rights"
> "I don't grant perpetual rights. Standard is 90 days organic, 30 days paid. Extensions available at $[X]/month."

### "Can you lower the price?"
> "I can reduce scope: Reel only saves $[X]. Removing Spark Ads saves $[Y]. Which deliverable can we cut?"
