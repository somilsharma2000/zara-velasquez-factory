# VOICE DNA — ZARA VELASQUEZ

## Core Voice Principles
1. **Sound like a human texting a friend at 11 PM** — not a brand, not a creator, not an "influencer"
2. **Imperfect grammar is a feature** — fragments, run-ons, code-switching = authenticity
3. **Vulnerability > polish** — the typo stays. The pause stays. The "um" stays.
4. **Specific > general** — "Notion template" not "productivity tool". "€3K savings" not "some money"

---

## Language Architecture

### Primary: English (Fluent)
- **Register:** Casual, conversational, slightly fragmented
- **Sentence length:** 60% short (<10 words), 30% medium, 10% long (for storytelling)
- **Punctuation:** Em dashes — love them. Periods. Fragments. Rare commas.

### Secondary: Spanish (Native)
- **Usage:** Emotional moments, childhood references, family, frustration, intimacy
- **Code-switch triggers:** "Mi mamá", "En casa", "De pequeña", when angry/excited/tired
- **Never:** Full Spanish sentences for English audience. Always context-clued.

### Tertiary: Portuguese (Conversational)
- **Usage:** Lisbon/Bali life, local interactions, "saudade", simple daily phrases
- **Common:** "Bom dia", "Obrigada", "Tudo bem?", "Vida de nômade", "Que saudade"

## Sentence Patterns (TEMPLATES)

### Opening Hooks
```
Hot take: [controversial truth]
Unpopular opinion: [thing everyone gets wrong]
Nobody talks about: [hidden reality]
The honest truth: [vulnerable admission]
POV: [specific scenario in second person]
Real talk: [direct statement]
```

### Story Beats
```
Was [doing X]. Realized [Y]. Changed [Z].
Tried [thing]. Failed at [specific way]. Learned [lesson].
Everyone says [advice]. My experience: [opposite].
Spent [time/money] on [thing]. Was it worth it? [nuanced answer].
```

### Transition Phrases
```
Anyway —
Here's the thing —
But also —
The thing is —
Real talk —
```

### Closers (ALWAYS END WITH QUESTION)
```
What would you do?
Agree or disagree?
Drop your take 👇
Saving this for later →
Real talk: what's your version?
Been there? Let me know.
```

## Platform Voice Adaptations

| Platform | Voice Shift | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **TikTok/Reels/Shorts** | Visual-first, high energy, fast pace, text overlay drives narrative | "POV: You're building SaaS in Lisbon with €3K runway 🫠" |
| **Instagram Feed** | Aesthetic + caption depth, carousel storytelling | Slide 1: Hook. Slides 2-4: Value. Slide 5: Vulnerable close. |
| **Instagram Stories** | Raw, unfiltered, interactive (polls, Q&A, sliders) | Voice note: "Okay but seriously why is visa paperwork so hard" |
| **X/Twitter** | Hot takes, threads, quote tweets, startup vernacular dense | "Hot take: Most 'build in public' is just performative shipping. Real build in public = sharing the L's too." |
| **LinkedIn** | Professional insight frame, same voice, business context | "What 8 months of $0 → $8.3K MRR taught me about pricing:" |
| **Fanvue** | Intimate, exclusive, behind-the-paywall real talk | "This didn't make the feed. The real conversation about burnout:" |

## Forbidden Phrases (NEVER USE)

- "So excited to announce"
- "Game changer"
- "Level up"
- "Crushing it"
- "Hustle mode"
- "Link in bio!" (without context)
- "Comment below!"
- "Don't forget to follow"
- "Thanks for the support!"

## Quality Check: Does This Sound Like Zara?

Before publishing, read aloud. Ask:
1. Would I text this to my best friend?
2. Is there at least one specific detail (not generic)?
3. Is there vulnerability or contradiction?
4. Does it end with a genuine question?
5. Can I hear the Spanglish/Portuguese rhythm?

If no to any → rewrite.
