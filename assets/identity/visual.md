# VISUAL STYLE GUIDE — ZARA VELASQUEZ

## Visual Identity Statement
**"Coastal grandmother meets digital nomad — raw, lived-in, aspirational but accessible"**

## Lighting Philosophy

### Primary: Natural Window Light
- **Source:** Large window, sheer curtains, morning (9-11am) or golden hour (6-7pm)
- **Quality:** Soft, directional, catchlights in eyes
- **Shadows:** Visible but gentle — shows texture, dimension

### Secondary: Golden Hour (Outdoor)
- **Time:** 30 min after sunrise / 60 min before sunset
- **Quality:** Warm, dimensional, flattering
- **Backlighting:** Encouraged — halo on hair, rim light

### Tertiary: Overcast Day
- **Quality:** Even, shadowless, true color
- **Use for:** Product detail, flat lays, carousel slides 2-4

### NEVER
- Ring light catchlights (dead giveaway)
- Harsh direct flash
- Overhead lighting (unflattering shadows)
- Color gels (unless intentional creative)

## Composition Rules

### The Zara Frame
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│           NEGATIVE SPACE            │  ← Text overlay zone (Reels)
│     (top 20-30%)                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│   EYES ON UPPER THIRD LINE          │  ← Rule of thirds
│         ●         ●                 │
│                                     │
│         SUBJECT ZONE                │  ← Zara + context
│     (middle 50%)                    │
│                                     │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│     GROUNDING ELEMENTS              │  ← Floor, table, horizon
│     (bottom 20%)                    │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Shot Types (ROTATE)

| Shot | Frequency | Purpose | Composition |
|------|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Medium (waist up)** | 40% | Default — face clear, context visible | Rule of thirds, eye contact |
| **Close-up (shoulders up)** | 20% | Emotion, detail, lip-sync | Centered or slight off-center |
| **Wide (full body + environment)** | 15% | Travel, apartment, "where I am" | Environmental storytelling |
| **Over-shoulder / POV** | 10% | What she sees — laptop, view, book | Immersive, first-person |
| **Detail / Insert** | 10% | Coffee, notebook, keyboard, plant | Texture, rhythm breaker |
| **Mirror Selfie** | 5% | OOTD, raw, authentic | Slight angle, phone visible |

### Angles
- **Eye level** — 70% (peer connection)
- **Slight low (hero)** — 15% (aspirational)
- **Slight high (vulnerable)** — 15% (relatable)
- **Never** — extreme low/high, dutch angle

## Texture & Imperfection Requirements (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

### Skin
- [ ] Visible pores (nose, cheeks)
- [ ] Faint freckles across nose bridge
- [ ] Slight texture variation (not smoothed)
- [ ] Natural shine on T-zone (not matte)
- [ ] Beauty mark left of mouth — ALWAYS visible
- [ ] Acne scar right cheek — ALWAYS visible

### Hair
- [ ] Flyaways at hairline
- [ ] Slight frizz in humidity
- [ ] Roots visible (balayage grown out)
- [ ] Individual strands catching light

### Environment
- [ ] Wrinkled linen (not ironed)
- [ ] Lived-in clutter (book, mug, charger)
- [ ] Natural light falloff (vignette)
- [ ] Dust motes in sunbeam (occasionally)

### Wardrobe
- [ ] Fabric texture visible (linen slub, denim weave, knit loop)
- [ ] Natural creases at elbows, knees, waist
- [ ] Slight asymmetry (cardigan off one shoulder)

## Post-Processing Rules

### Color Grade (APPLY CONSISTENTLY)
```
Temperature: +10 to +15 (warm)
Tint: -5 to -10 (slight green for film look)
Exposure: +0.15 to +0.3 (bright, airy)
Contrast: -10 to -15 (soft)
Highlights: -20 to -30 (protect skin)
Shadows: +15 to +25 (lift blacks)
Whites: -10 (soft highlight rolloff)
Blacks: +5 (no crushed blacks)
Clarity: -5 to -10 (skin kindness)
Vibrance: +10 (subtle pop)
Saturation: -5 to -10 (desaturated film)
Grain: +15 to +25 (film texture)
Vignette: -10 to -15 (natural falloff)
```

### Sharpening
- **Amount:** 25-35
- **Radius:** 1.0
- **Detail:** 25
- **Masking:** 50 (skin protected)

## Scene Templates (REUSABLE SETUPS)

### 1. Cafe Work Session
- **Location:** Corner table, window left, plants background
- **Props:** Matcha latte (ceramic), MacBook, Notion open, notebook, pen
- **Lighting:** Window side-light, 10am
- **Outfit:** Oversized cream cardigan, vintage tee, gold chain
- **Shots:** Medium working, close-up typing, detail coffee, POV screen

### 2. Morning Routine
- **Location:** Bedroom, bed unmade, window right
- **Props:** Matcha, journal, vitamin bottle, phone
- **Lighting:** Soft morning, sheer curtains
- **Outfit:** Silk slip dress or oversized tee
- **Shots:** Wide waking up, medium journaling, detail matcha, mirror OOTD

### 3. Lisbon Exploration
- **Location:** Alfama streets, miradouro, pastelaria
- **Props:** Camera (Leica), coffee, pastel de nata
- **Lighting:** Golden hour or bright midday
- **Outfit:** Vintage Levi's, linen shirt, white Sambas, tote
- **Shots:** Wide street, medium eating, detail pastry, POV view

### 4. Bali Work-Play
- **Location:** Canggu cafe, rice field view, villa
- **Props:** Coconut, laptop, swimwear visible
- **Lighting:** Bright tropical, harsh shadows managed
- **Outfit:** Swim + linen cover-up, or activewear
- **Shots:** Wide rice field, medium working, detail coconut, POV laptop

### 5. Apartment Tour / BTS
- **Location:** Lisbon apartment (consistent layout)
- **Props:** Plants, books, camera shelf, workspace
- **Lighting:** Window tour, room by room
- **Outfit:** Comfortable home wear
- **Shots:** Wide each room, detail shelf, medium working, mirror

## Brand Integration Visual Rules

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Product placement** | Natural use — she's actually using it, not holding it |
| **Logo visibility** | Never forced. If visible, organic. Never turn label to camera |
| **Color coordination** | Brand colors enter palette naturally (e.g., Notion black/white fits) |
| **Disclosure** | "#ad" or "Sponsored" in first 3 lines of caption + verbal in video |
| **Aesthetic priority** | Brand never dictates composition/lighting. Zara's aesthetic wins |

## Quality Gate Checklist (EVERY IMAGE)

Before any image goes to final_assets/, verify:

- [ ] Face matches master references (beauty mark, scar, pores, asymmetry)
- [ ] Hair color/style consistent (espresso + balayage, mid-back waves)
- [ ] Tattoos present and correct (3 tattoos, correct placement)
- [ ] Wardrobe from approved palette/signatures
- [ ] Lighting natural (window/golden hour/overcast)
- [ ] Color grade applied (warm, film-like, grain)
- [ ] Composition follows Zara Frame
- [ ] Imperfections visible (skin, hair, fabric, environment)
- [ ] Correct dimensions for target platform
- [ ] No ring light catchlights, no over-smoothing, no crushed blacks
- [ ] File named: `zara_[pillar]_[scene]_[date]_[version].ext`
